# F2.5 Footprint naming conventions for specific components

Source: <https://klc.kicad.org/footprint/f2/f2.5/> (KLC 3.0.67, group F2 General Footprint Naming Guidelines)

Footprint naming is sufficiently complex that a general rule is not enough to fully define a naming scheme that fits the wide range of footprints.

In addition to the general footprint naming guidelines defined in [KLC 2.1](F2.1.md), a set of footprint naming guidelines for specific footprint types is provided in [KLC F3.x](https://klc.kicad.org/footprint/f3).
