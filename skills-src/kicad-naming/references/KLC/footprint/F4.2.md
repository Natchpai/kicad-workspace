# F4.2 Pin 1 should be located at the top left

Source: <https://klc.kicad.org/footprint/f4/f4.2/> (KLC 3.0.67, group F4 General Footprint Requirements)

Footprints should be oriented such that Pin 1 is located in the upper left corner (IPC-7351).

![Pin 1 aligned at top-left corner](https://klc.kicad.org/img/klc/F4.2_pin1_top_left.png)

When the pin cannot be the most top-left pin in the footprint, it should be in the top-left quadrant of the footprint.

![Pin 1 aligned at top-left quadrant](https://klc.kicad.org/img/klc/F4.2_pin1_top_left_2.png)

**Exceptions**

Two-terminal footprints and other footprints with all pins in a single line should be aligned with pin 1 on the left side.

![Two-terminal and inline footprints align pin 1 on left](https://klc.kicad.org/img/klc/F4.2_inline_pins.png)

Multi-purpose connector footprints should be oriented horizontally, with pin 1 on the left side,
for easier comparison and alignment with the matching connector.

![Connector headers align pin 1 on left](https://klc.kicad.org/img/klc/F4.2_connector_orientation.png)

Footprints for packages with pin 1 located in the middle of one side (e.g. some PLCC and QFN packages)
should have pin 1 aligned to the top side.

![Align to top if pin 1 in the middle of a side](https://klc.kicad.org/img/klc/F4.2_pin1_at_top.png)
